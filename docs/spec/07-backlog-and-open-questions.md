# A. Major Story Questions

## 1. Who exactly is the client?

Current surface truth:

- ordinary person
- missing sibling
- credible emotional history

Open possibilities:

- corporate connection
- government connection
- foreign intelligence connection
- artificial person
- facility operative
- activation mechanism for the protagonist
- alien or nonhuman connection

## 2. What happened to the missing sibling?

Possibilities include:

- kidnapped
- deleted
- reprogrammed
- replaced
- re-instantiated
- escaped
- infiltrated facility
- double agent
- intentionally disappeared

Likely answer may combine several.

## 3. What exactly is the protagonist?

Possibilities:

- human investigator
- android
- artificial person
- diagnostic agent
- simulated consciousness
- copy of another person
- intentionally ambiguous hybrid

## 4. Why is the protagonist unusually resistant to memory changes?

Possibilities:

- head injury
- different memory architecture
- offline restoration
- special investigator role
- old firmware
- deliberate protection
- family artifact
- something Dad did

## 5. What is the true nature of the underground facility?

Potential layers:

- corporate infrastructure
- nuclear support
- government research
- artificial-person maintenance
- simulation infrastructure
- system metaphor
- physical and computational layers overlapping

## 6. Is there an original reality?

Recommended default:

Do not answer definitively unless the story demands it.

# G. Structural Questions

## 1. Exact protagonist behavioral dimensions

Current:

- analytical
- social
- direct/operator

Could evolve.

## 2. How visible should the profile become?

Possibilities:

- hidden throughout
- exposed midway
- shown by terminal
- player can inspect late

## 3. How much time pressure?

Inspired by *Deadline*, but avoid brittle exact timing.

Potential:

- soft schedules
- event windows
- NPC movement
- no hard move-count cruelty

## 4. How many major endings?

Current preference:

- one major canonical recursive ending
- path differences affect understanding and route
- avoid dozens of endings
