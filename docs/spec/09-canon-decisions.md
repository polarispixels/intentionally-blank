  everything from Act IV down gets rewritten. Cheapest possible moment to
  reverse it is now.

  into the Mars sky reveal; Catan stays as flavor). The reasoning is scope:
  the architecture already prices at the 30k-word ceiling. All remain
  available to post-1.0 expansion.
