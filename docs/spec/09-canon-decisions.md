## Stage A promotion (entries 3–17)

Entries 3–17 were proposed by `game-architect` in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-stage-a-story-architecture.md` §8 and
accepted by the main session on 2026-08-29 under the full-game build
protocol. They are listed in the architect's blast-radius order, so entry 3
(what the protagonist is) is the one whose reversal is most expensive and
therefore the one worth reading first.

Two are flagged for Ryan's attention above the others:

- **Entry 3 (A1)** — the investigator is a subject Jules created. This is
  the decision the back half of the game is built on; if it is wrong,
  everything from Act IV down gets rewritten. Cheapest possible moment to
  reverse it is now.
- **Entry 9 (A7)** — Washington DC, Mount Rushmore, Puerto Rico, the
  distant station, and a playable Catan sequence are cut **as locations**.
  Every reference survives in compressed form (Luke visits the facility;
  Rushmore is a postcard carrying a Mandela beat aimed at the player's own
  memory; Puerto Rico is a circular-ownership paper clue; the station folds
  into the Mars sky reveal; Catan stays as flavor). The reasoning is scope:
  the architecture already prices at the 30k-word ceiling. All remain
  available to post-1.0 expansion.

Decisions made by subagents are proposals until the main session enters
them here.
