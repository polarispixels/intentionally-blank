# 09 - Canon Decisions Register

**Spec version:** `0.2.5`

During the full-game build (see `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`, "Full-game build
protocol") the main session is authorized to resolve `WORKING IDEA` and
`POSSIBILITY` items and promote them to `CANON` in docs 02–07, recording
every such decision here. Ryan reviews this register at his discretion and
may reverse any entry; a reversal is a story rewrite, so sooner is cheaper.

Each entry: the question, the decision, why (what it enables or makes
consistent), what it forecloses, and where it now lives in the spec.

| # | Date | Question (source) | Decision | Why | Forecloses | Now in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-08-29 | What are the five siblings' names? (03 §3 — previously numerals only) | I **Jules**, II **Luke**, III **Eli**, IV **Jack**, V **Sissy** | Ryan's decision. Named siblings are required before any NPC dialogue, memory fragment, or tattoo puzzle can be written; numerals alone cannot carry prose. | Alternative names; any reading where the numerals are the characters' only identifiers. | `03 §3`, `03 §9`, `03 §10` |
| 2 | 2026-08-29 | Which sibling is the missing facilities supervisor? (03 §4 — unnamed) | **Jules**, sibling I | Forced by elimination: Luke, Eli, Jack, and Sissy already have canon 2047 occupations, and the supervisor is a fifth. Making the *oldest* the missing one also sharpens the family stakes — the client is looking for the sibling who was supposed to be the reliable one. | The supervisor being Sissy (which would have put the only sister at the data center and removed the Mars bridge); any later reveal that the supervisor is a sixth, forgotten sibling — though "the family remembers five" remains available as an erasure beat about *someone else*. | `03 §3`, `03 §4` |

Decisions made by subagents are proposals until the main session enters
them here.
