## 1. The spine, per wave (constitution §29)

Stage D's last link: *THEREFORE the question stops being "what happened to Jules" and becomes "what are these people — and what am I?"* Each wave states its chain from the previous wave's last link; every room enters with its causal reason (§32) in its wave table.

**E0.** The question is about the self — **THEREFORE** the evidence about the self is gathered where it already lies: the sheriff's cage (his own case notes, in his own hand, next to Jules's notebook), the Hub's ledger (which takes a numeral as a key and files two subjects under `I`), the Hub's terminal (which has been scoring every move since turn one and now says so) — **BUT** each piece has a mundane dodge and dodges are not answers; and every credential in the game stops at the root door — somebody must outrank the building — **THEREFORE** the man who can: the President is coming to the facility his family's administration commissioned, and the town has been repaving for him.

**E1.** The President is coming — **BUT** the investigator has no identity, and the detail is trained to see exactly this kind of nobody coming; every credential he holds is fake because he is — **THEREFORE** the family channel, composed of everything Act II taught: folded the way Eli folds, worded the way Luke words, signed with a numeral, handed by the one person on the route the detail doesn't screen. Luke reads it. Luke stops the motorcade — and shows the letters "from Jack" that Jack never wrote (R15) — **BUT** even the President's authority bottoms out at Sublevel 6: the two-thing door opens for him, and the root door's reader was never switched on (R16).

**E2.** Authority is the wrong axis — **THEREFORE** the remaining doors are family doors: the archive's reconstructed last day together, which only completes for the one who remembers Jules's part — and he does, perfectly (P23, R14's undodgeable form); and Sissy's sky, which cannot be rewritten in transit if it never transits: chemical film, developed by hand, and it is the porch Polaroid's sky with the seams showing (P24, R17) — **BUT** knowing Jules's memories is not being Jules — the difference is a creation record, and creation records live at root — and the sky proves there is no outside to escalate to.

**E3.** Every road up is rendered — **THEREFORE** the only true direction is down: Dad's shaft by the tunnel branch, or Luke's cleared stair, and at the antechamber the credentials that have been in the back cover all along — accepted, as they would have been all along (R18) — **THEREFORE** the record: `AUTHOR: JULES I` (R19); and, for the player who assembled the deep index, Jules himself, woken once (R20) — **BUT** the reconciliation queued on Sublevel 6 is running, and nothing addressable survives it — **THEREFORE** the family's method, one generation deeper: put the truth where the system cannot reach, and at the console do the one thing reconciliation cannot touch, because a blank is not addressable. `INITIALIZE?` `> YES`. Darkness. Your head hurts. *(Hands to the opening room.)*

### 3.1 ADR 0012 — the ending that continues (draft decision text)

> **ADR 0012: The recursive ending continues into the opening room; nothing crosses it**
>
> **Context.** Canon 02 §19 ends the game with `INITIALIZE? / > YES / Darkness. / Your head hurts.` followed by the opening room — the player performs the revelation; no monologue, no menu, no system line. The shipped session has `{ end }` (phase `'ended'`, input refused) and `restart` (a fresh `createSession` behind a confirm prompt or the death menu), and both shells treat a restart as a new game with a visible break. Neither is the ending. The `'auto'` slot would hold the fresh opening one turn after the hand-off, so a player who finished could never re-read the console. A history that crossed the hand-off would replay the next game's inputs against the wrong state.
>
> **Decision.** (1) `WorldMeta.recursiveEnding?: string` names the one ending id the session treats as a hand-off; the validator warns (`meta-recursive-ending-unreferenced`) when no `{ end }` effect or script in the world carries it. (2) When `takeTurn` or `respondToPrompt` observes `{ type: 'ended', endingId === meta.recursiveEnding }`, the session — not the shell — writes the ended state to the reserved slot `'ending'`, removes slots `'undo'` and `'checkpoint'`, starts a fresh game (`startSession`), writes it to `'auto'`, and returns the fresh session with the events of both: the turn's events **minus the `ended` event**, then the opening arrival's. No `restarted` event is emitted (that event clears shell transcripts). `RespondToPromptResult`/`TakeTurnResult` gain `handedOff?: true`. `respondToPrompt` takes an optional `PersistOptions`; without it the hand-off still happens in memory and nothing is written (fixtures). (3) `'ending'` joins the reserved slots: never listed, loadable by `LOAD ending`, which resumes with `phase: 'ended'`. (4) `SaveStore` gains `remove(slot)`; `MemoryStore`, `FileStore`, `LocalStorageStore` implement it. (5) The replay invariant is **per cycle**: the fresh session starts a new `history`; nothing — no flag, no counter, no save field — crosses the hand-off (register 99). (6) Movement verbs (the twelve `DIRECTION_VERB_IDS` and `GO TO`) are `class: null`; the profile tallies choices, not logistics (register 100). (7) `system.buildBoundary` is retired in two steps: E0 renames the road gate off the `*_boundary_gate` pattern and the count test asserts one; E3 deletes the last gate and the test asserts zero. No `END OF BUILD` string may exist in content at 1.0.
>
> **Consequences.** The CLI and the Vue shell need no ending logic of their own: they render an event stream that happens to end one game and begin another. A transcript of a full game is one file. `UNDO` after the hand-off says there is nothing to undo; `RESTART ENCOUNTER` says there is no checkpoint. `saveVersion` stays 1. A second playthrough is the same game with a player who knows more (spec 04 §14); the opening terminal's `LOG IN` is content, not a mechanic. Rejected: a shell-side `restarted`-style hook (the two shells would disagree, as they did over RESTART until `parseMetaCommand`); a cycle counter or NEW GAME+ flag (register 99); a `saveVersion` bump (nothing in the envelope changes); leaving the `'undo'` slot in place (one `UNDO` would jump from the opening room back into the ending).
