# Stage E Wave E3 — Prose Addendum: the composed letter

**Status:** commissioned and approved by the main session 2026-09-20; wired
same change. **Author:** `narrative-writer` · **Covers:** `act2_letter_out`'s
missing `READ`/`EXAMINE` (found by the E3 integration playtest — the engine's
READ fallback crashed on the object). Constitution §31 holds absolutely: the
player's own words are never printed, paraphrased, or counted.

## 1. EXAMINE — two arms

`when: { objectState: [act2_letter_out, 'folded', true] }` (the prop is only
ever true after M13, so a true value always means the origami fold):

```text
Folded, it is the size of a playing card and weighs about as much as one.
No envelope, no gum, no string: over, under, the hard crease along the
third, and everything you had to say is shut inside paper by paper alone.

The box number ended up on the outside, upside down. The fold did not
consult you.
```

unconditional:

```text
The free paper off the back of the rack, thin enough that the pen has come
through in the places where you leaned on it. The lines start level and
end up running downhill toward the right margin, which is where lines go
when a man stops watching them.

On the back, the number of a box in this building, in the same pen and a
slower hand.
```

## 2. READ — one arm, geometry-neutral

```text
You read it back, and it is worse coming out than it was going in, which
is what second readings are for.

The hand is small and quick and slopes to the right. It is yours. You do
not remember learning it. It went on being yours anyway.
```

> **Note.** *You do not remember learning it* is the load-bearing line —
> amnesia plus muscle memory now; the same shape as the M13 fold on a second
> reading. No comparison of hands, no naming of whose hand it also is
> (canon 33, E0 §15's closed comparisons).
