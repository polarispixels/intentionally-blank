**Status:** proposed by `game-architect` (Fable) 2026-08-31 · awaiting main-session review and Ryan's decision
**Trigger:** the shipped opening slice measured ~11,000 player-visible words for 2 rooms (~3,700/room net of the ~3,600-word one-time global response families). At that density the 41-room plan projects ~150,000 words — 5–7× the spec 00 target of 20–30k.

## 0. The principle of the cut

Density cuts come out of **breadth, not anticipation**. Constitution §14's quality — "someone thought about what would happen if I tried this" — is per-object depth, and it is protected absolutely: every object that survives keeps its full authored treatment (examine, the verbs its nature invites, at least one state variant where state exists). What shrinks is the *count* of addressable objects per room and the count of rooms. A room with six well-answered objects beats one with fifteen thin ones.

Room cuts come out of **geography, not the puzzle network**. Every cut below removes walls, not puzzles: all 28 puzzles (P1–P28) survive, two of them restaged (P15's venue, P9's travel delivery). No reveal (R1–R21), no ledger line (L1–L20), and no memory trigger loses its home without an explicit forwarding address in §4.

## 2. Density tiers

The tier is the instruction. "Write less" is not actionable; an object count and a word ceiling are. Every room in §1 carries exactly one tier. `narrative-writer` receives the tier with the room brief and writes *to* it.

| Tier | Rooms | Word target (all-in: description + state variants + objects + room-specific responses) | Concrete shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hero** (4) | Your Room (shipped) · S6 Maintenance Bay · Escape Room Chamber · The Blank Room | **~3,000, ceiling 3,700** (the shipped opening's actual) | 10–14 addressable objects, full state-variant coverage, senses suite, a secret, 10+ room-specific reasonable-action responses. The opening-room prose spec is the template. |
| **Standard** (24) | Everything not listed in the other rows | **~1,200 typical, 1,400 ceiling** | 5–7 addressable objects, each fully answered (examine + the verbs its nature invites + one state variant where state exists); 4–6 room-specific responses; everything else falls through to the global response families, which exist precisely so ordinary rooms don't re-answer the world. |
| **Light / transit** (4) | Pipe Chase · Service Tunnel · Root Shaft · Root Antechamber | **~400–600** | 2–4 objects, description, 2–3 responses. These rooms are movement with one idea each. |

Why these four heroes: the opening is shipped and is the bar; the Maintenance Bay is the midpoint detonation's floor (R9–R12 land on it and the player must *believe* the room); the Chamber is the identity thread's proof and the game's one memory-as-capability set piece; the Blank Room is the ending. Each hero sits at an act boundary where the player will re-read everything. Wall Drug Emporium, Jack's Motel, the Archive Hub, and the Observation Dome are **standard tier with a polish-priority flag** — same budget, first claim on revision passes.

Two riders:
- **The Upstairs Landing shipped at ~hero density for a corridor.** Do not rewrite it now (it's shipped and good); it is the named donor for a later trim pass (~−1,500–2,000 words) if the running total drifts above budget. Ryan's "refactor further later" applies here first.
- **Non-room text is budgeted separately** (§5): global families (shipped), NPC dialogue, memory fragments, terminal/system text. Room tiers cover the room and its objects only. The Archive Hub ledger and the Blank Room console live mostly in the terminal budget by design.

## 7. What this deliberately does not do

- No puzzle, reveal, NPC, or memory fragment is cut. If the word count is still high after Act I content lands, the next levers are (in order): the Landing trim, the reserved M21–24 replay fragments, and dialogue depth for Pearl/Dot — never the network.
- No engine change is required. Scenes exist (the prologue uses them); connectors are exits with prose; state-variant rooms are already an engine requirement (Appendix item 2 of the story architecture).
- The cut rooms are **marked cut, not deleted**: Highway 240, the Café, the Gatehouse, S3, the Antechamber, the Airlock, the Alley, the Forecourt, the Back Room, and the Freight Elevator all remain in the Stage A document as the post-1.0 restoration list (07 I).
- Shipped content is not rewritten. The opening room and landing stand as-is; the Landing trim is a named reserve, not a task.
